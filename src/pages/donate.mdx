## Where your money goes

- **Hosting & CI** — keeps the [Velaptor Docs Website](https://docs.velaptor.io) and the release pipelines running.
- **Hardware** — testing across Windows, Linux, and macOS.
- **Focused dev time** — the hours that turn issues into releases.

:::tip[One-time is great. Monthly is gold.]
One-time gifts are always appreciated, but monthly sponsorships give the project predictable
funding — and predictable funding is what makes planned releases possible.
:::
